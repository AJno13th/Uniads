**The trap in this post, and why it's avoided.** The obvious next line is "and
you'll earn more with a degree". That would be an employment-outcome claim under
CAP 20.9 and needs a cited, dated source. There is deliberately no earnings claim
anywhere in today's three posts — the arithmetic is about the viewer's own age and
nothing else. If this angle is extended later, the salary figure must come from ONS
or the National Careers Service with the source on screen.

**On "no upper age limit":** accurate for tuition fee loans for undergraduate
study, and it is a statutory fact rather than a UNIADS feature, so it is stated
plainly and not presented as something UNIADS provides (CAP 3.12). Note separately
that under the LLE system from January 2027 you must be **under 60** on day one to
apply for a tuition fee loan — so do not use the "no upper age limit" line
alongside a January 2027 intake, and never with anyone approaching 60.

**Escalation flag:** "I'm 58, is it worth it?" — genuine question, needs a human,
and the under-60 LLE rule is directly relevant. Do not answer in comments.
