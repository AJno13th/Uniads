**Note on "several of our partner universities":** this wording is deliberate and
must not be upgraded to "our universities" or "all our partners". Per
`src/data/universities.ts`, five partners advertise no formal qualification
requirement — GBS, LSST, UWTSD CertHE routes, Arden CertHE routes and London Met
— while the rest require a Level 3 or evidenced work experience. "Our partner
universities accept you without qualifications" would be false for most of the
list.

**Escalation flag:** "which uni would take me?" in the comments. Don't name one
publicly against a stranger's circumstances — move it to DM.
