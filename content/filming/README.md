# Production standards

Read this once before the first shoot. It is deliberately low-tech: everything
here is achievable with a modern phone, and the evidence says production value is
not what decides performance in this niche.

## Why this is filmed in batches

The measured pattern from [../research/competitor-teardown.md](../research/competitor-teardown.md):
one account in this niche has posted **438 videos** and its recent post got **832
views**. Another posted **51** and averages 14x the likes per video. Volume is not
the lever; the hook and the edit are.

So: film a small number of things properly, in two sessions, and cut each into
several posts. You never film to a deadline, and no post ever ships because
something had to go out today.

## The three craft rules

Derived from actual numbers, not taste.

1. **12–20 seconds.** The one six-figure video in our sample was 12 seconds. The
   two that died were 22s and 50s.
2. **One idea per video.** If a script has two ideas, it is two videos.
3. **Write for the person who forwards it.** That video earned 614 shares against
   32 comments — about 19:1. Distribution here comes from someone sending it to a
   person who needs to see it, not from argument in the replies.

## Camera

- Phone, rear camera, **4K 30fps**, vertical 9:16
- Lock exposure and focus before the take (tap and hold)
- Phone at eye height, roughly an arm and a half away
- Never zoom while recording
- Clean the lens — genuinely the most common cause of soft footage

## Framing

- Eyes on the **top third** line
- Chest-up for most blocks; shoulders-up for SB-10; slightly wider for SB-03 and SB-09
- Leave headroom for a caption but keep the bottom third clear — TikTok's own UI covers it
- Sit or stand still. No walking-and-talking.

## Audio

Audio decides whether people stay, far more than picture.

- Record in a room with soft furnishings; avoid kitchens, bathrooms, stairwells
- Phone mic is acceptable within about 60cm; a £20 lav mic is better
- Turn off any fan, extractor or aircon and close the window
- **Clap once at the start of every take** so audio and picture can be realigned if needed
- Film one 10-second room-tone take per session for the editor

## Light

- Face a window. Never have the window behind you.
- Overcast days are ideal; direct sun through glass is not
- No overhead ceiling light as the only source — it puts shadows under the eyes
- Once set, don't move between takes in a block, so cut-downs match

## Delivery

- One take per file, no in-camera edits
- Do not add TikTok captions or effects in-app — burn text in at the edit so the same footage can be reused across all three platforms
- Export cut-downs at 1080x1920, H.264, under 60MB

## File naming

```

SB-02_age-30_take-01_12s.mp4
SB-05_ceiling_take-02_17s.mp4

```

Store as:

```
    /SB-02

    /broll
  /session-2
  /ugc
```

## On-screen text

- Burn in at the edit, not in-app
- Sans-serif, bold, heavy weight, white with a dark drop shadow or a solid block behind
- Maximum ~6 words per frame
- First frame on screen within 0.5s — it carries the hook for everyone watching muted
- Keep clear of the bottom third and the right-hand rail

## Sound and music

- Talking-head blocks: original audio, no music bed
- For carousels and b-roll cutdowns: pick a **trending sound at low volume** on the day you post. Trending audio can't be planned in advance, which is a reason to leave it to the daily step.
- Never put music over a block containing funding figures — the numbers must be audible

## Subtitles

Burn in subtitles on **every** video. Most of this audience watches muted, and the
content is information-dense. Auto-generate, then fix every number by hand against
[../brand-facts.md](../brand-facts.md) — auto-captions mangle figures like
£199.62 and £14,135, and a wrong number on screen is a compliance problem even if
the audio is right.

## On the day

- Have [../guardrails.md](../guardrails.md) open
- Read scripts as written. If a line feels wrong, **cut it and note it** — do not improvise a replacement fact.
- Minimum 3 takes per script; the third is usually the one
- Check the first take back with headphones before shooting the rest
- Figures in SB-05 and SB-07 are read off the printed GOV.UK page, not from memory
