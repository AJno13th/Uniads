# Shoot blocks — batch production pack

Twelve blocks, filmed across two sessions, cut into roughly a month of posts.
This exists so you never have to film on a deadline: everything that needs a
camera is specified here up front, and the daily posts draw on footage you
already have.

Read [README.md](README.md) for framing, audio and delivery specs before you
start, and [shoot-schedule.md](shoot-schedule.md) for the running order.

**On camera:** Anthony Joshua, named as the counsellor. That is deliberate — the
recurring theme in competitors' verified reviews is a single trusted named
advisor, and UNIADS is the only one in this set that can pair a name with a
publicly verifiable British Council training badge.

**Every script below has been checked against
[../guardrails.md](../guardrails.md).** Do not ad-lib new facts on the day. If a
line feels wrong while filming, cut it and note it — don't improvise a
replacement claim.

## SB-01 — "No GCSEs? No A-Levels? No problem."

**Evidence:** Tier 1.1. The most validated hook in the category — a competitor has
run this hook continuously since 9 February 2026 across 8+4 creatives, and it
appears in their ads as far back as October 2025.

**Feeds:** 4 posts (TikTok hero, IG Reel, FB video, IG carousel cover)

**Setup**
- Location: plain wall, indoors, window to camera-left
- Framing: vertical 9:16, mid-chest to just above head, eyes on the top third
- Wardrobe: shirt, no logo, no busy pattern
- Props: none
- Takes: 3 minimum, vary energy on take 3

**Script — 16 seconds**

| Time | Line |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00 | "No GCSEs. No A-Levels. You can still start a degree this year." |
| 0:04 | "If you're 21 or over, several of our partner universities will look at your work experience instead — an interview, or a short assessment." |
| 0:11 | "If you're 18 to 21, you'll usually need a Level 3." |
| 0:14 | "That's it. That's the whole requirement." |

**On-screen text frames**
1. `0:00` — NO GCSEs. NO A-LEVELS.
2. `0:04` — 21+ → work experience counts
3. `0:11` — 18–21 → Level 3 needed
4. `0:14` — uniads.co.uk

**B-roll:** none needed. Keep it a straight talking head — it is the single
clearest statement of the offer and the face is the point.

**Cut-downs**
- **A (12s):** first two lines only, hard cut on "assessment"
- **B (16s):** full
- **C (8s):** "If you're 21 or over, you don't need qualifications to start a degree. You need work experience and an interview." — for FB where dwell is shorter

## SB-02 — The age series

**Evidence:** Tier 3.1. Taken from competitor SEO targeting, where the search
volume actually is: `study-in-the-uk-at-21`, `how-to-apply-university-at-30-uk`,
`uk-business-degree-for-adult-learners`,
`best-university-courses-for-mature-students-uk`. People search their own age,
not the phrase "mature student".

**Feeds:** 5 posts — one per age, all from one setup

**Setup**
- Location: same wall as SB-01, change shirt so it doesn't look like one video sliced up
- Framing: as SB-01
- Props: none
- Takes: film all five ages back to back, then repeat the set

**Script — 5 variants, 12 seconds each**

Base structure, swap the numbers:

> "You're **[AGE]**. You think it's too late. Let's do the maths.
> Four years including a foundation year — you graduate at **[AGE+4]**.
> If you work to 67, that's **[67-(AGE+4)]** more years with a degree behind you.
> Still feel late?"

| Age | Graduates at | Working years after |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 25 | 29 | 38 |
| 30 | 34 | 33 |
| 35 | 39 | 28 |
| 40 | 44 | 23 |
| 45 | 49 | 18 |

**On-screen text frames**
1. `0:00` — "I'm [AGE]. Is it too late?"
2. `0:03` — Graduate at [AGE+4]
3. `0:07` — [N] working years. With a degree.
4. `0:11` — Still feel late?

**B-roll:** optional single cutaway of a hand writing the two numbers on paper.

**Compliance note:** this is arithmetic about the viewer's own age, not a claim
about earnings or employment. Do **not** add "and you'll earn more" — that would
need a cited source under CAP 20.9.

## SB-04 — What a CertHE, HND and foundation year actually are

**Evidence:** Tier 1.2. A competitor runs three concurrent time-compression
angles, including "🎓 Uni Qualification in just one year?" (live from 16 May 2026,
8 creatives) and "why are thousands of working adults suddenly doing this one-year
uni course?" (live from 9 February 2026, 8 creatives).

**Feeds:** 4 posts (one per qualification, one comparison)

**Setup**
- Location: desk, seated, laptop closed, notepad visible
- Framing: chest up, slightly off-centre so text can sit beside you
- Props: three index cards labelled CertHE / HND / Foundation Year
- Takes: 2 per card

**Script — CertHE, 15 seconds**

| Time | Line |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00 | "One year at university, and you walk away with a qualification either way." |
| 0:04 | "It's called a CertHE — a Certificate of Higher Education." |
| 0:07 | "Finish it and you can carry on into a degree, or stop there and still hold it." |
| 0:12 | "Some of our partners take you onto one at 18, with no qualifications." |

**Script — HND, 14 seconds**

| Time | Line |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00 | "An HND is two years, and it's worth the first two years of a degree." |
| 0:05 | "A Higher National Diploma. You can top it up to a full bachelor's with one more year." |
| 0:11 | "That top-up is a separate application — it isn't automatic." |

**Script — Foundation year, 14 seconds**

| Time | Line |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00 | "A foundation year isn't a year before university. It's year one *of* university." |
| 0:05 | "It's attached to the degree, so you're in from day one and go straight through." |
| 0:10 | "For a classroom-based subject, the tuition fee loan for that year is up to £5,760." |

**On-screen text frames**
- CertHE — `CertHE = 1 year` / `Certificate of Higher Education` / `Keep it, or continue`
- HND — `HND = 2 years` / `= years 1 & 2 of a degree` / `Top-up = separate application`
- Foundation — `Foundation year = year 0, attached` / `Tuition fee loan up to £5,760` / `classroom-based subjects`

**Compliance note:** naming the award accurately is the whole point of this block —
the ASA ruled against London School of Planning and Management for advertising an
"MBA" that was a Level 7 Diploma. Never shorten CertHE or HND to "a degree", and
always state that the HND top-up is a separate application.

## SB-05 — The real numbers

**Evidence:** Tier 4 for the hook, but the funding facts are GOV.UK and this is
UNIADS' clearest differentiator. One competitor advertises "up to £16,000 per
year" — above every published maximum. Accuracy is a position competitors cannot
copy without correcting themselves.

**Feeds:** 4 posts

**Setup**
- Location: desk, seated
- Framing: chest up, space to the right for large figures
- Props: printed GOV.UK page on the desk, visible but not readable
- Takes: 2 per variant. Read the figures off the page — get them exactly right

**Script — the ceiling, 17 seconds**

| Time | Line |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00 | "You might have seen people quote sixteen thousand a year in maintenance loan." |
| 0:04 | "The published maximum for twenty-six/twenty-seven is fourteen thousand, one hundred and thirty-five — and that's living away from home, in London." |
| 0:11 | "Outside London it's ten thousand, eight hundred and thirty." |
| 0:14 | "And it's means-tested, so most people get less. Ask for the real number." |

**Script — tuition, 13 seconds**

| Time | Line |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00 | "Tuition isn't free. Let's be straight about that." |
| 0:03 | "There's a tuition fee loan — up to nine thousand, seven hundred and ninety for twenty-six/twenty-seven — paid straight to the university." |
| 0:09 | "You repay it later, once you're earning above the threshold. Our help is the free part." |

**On-screen text frames**
- Ceiling: `£16,000?` → `Max £14,135` `(London, away from home, 26/27)` → `£10,830 outside London` → `Means-tested. Source: GOV.UK`
- Tuition: `Tuition is NOT free` → `Tuition fee loan up to £9,790` → `Paid to the uni. You repay it.` → `Our support is the free part`

**B-roll:** screen recording scrolling gov.uk/student-finance/new-fulltime-students
to the maintenance loan table. Film this — it is the proof and it makes the
accuracy claim land.

**Compliance note:** never name the competitor. "You might have seen people quote"
is the approved framing. Every figure needs "up to" and the means-tested
qualifier.

## SB-06 — Do you actually qualify for funding?

**Evidence:** Tier 3.2. The genuinely under-served question in this market.
Competitors sell the place; almost nobody explains the funding gate properly.
UNIADS already collects exactly these signals in its lead qualifier.

**Feeds:** 4 posts

**Setup**
- Location: desk
- Framing: chest up
- Props: none — resist the urge to wave documents containing real data
- Takes: 2 per variant

**Script — the three-year rule, 16 seconds**

| Time | Line |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00 | "Everyone asks about grades. The thing that actually decides your funding is where you've been living." |
| 0:05 | "For full support you normally need three years' continuous residence in the UK before your course starts." |
| 0:11 | "Plus an eligible status. Both, not either." |
| 0:14 | "If you're not sure where you stand, ask before you apply." |

**Script — statuses, 16 seconds**

| Time | Line |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00 | "Which immigration statuses can get student finance?" |
| 0:03 | "British and Irish citizens. Indefinite leave to remain. EU settled status. Refugee status, humanitarian protection, the Ukraine scheme." |
| 0:10 | "Pre-settled status and dependants can qualify in some cases — that one genuinely depends on your circumstances." |
| 0:14 | "We check it properly before you apply. We don't guess." |

**On-screen text frames**
- Three-year: `Not your grades.` → `3 years' continuous UK residence` → `+ eligible status` → `Both. Not either.`
- Statuses: list the statuses as a plain vertical stack, then `Pre-settled / dependants → depends` then `We check before you apply`

**Compliance note:** the closing line must always be that eligibility is checked,
never that the viewer qualifies. See guardrails 2.10.

## SB-07 — Student parents

**Evidence:** Tier 3.5. Childcare Grant plus Parents' Learning Allowance are real,
specific, non-repayable entitlements that competitors barely mention — one buries
it in an FAQ, the other omits it entirely. UNIADS has a whole service page for it.

**Feeds:** 3 posts. Expect this to be the best-performing block — it names a
concrete blocker and answers it with money that isn't repaid.

**Setup**
- Location: softer setting — sofa, kitchen table, home-adjacent
- Framing: chest up, warmer, slower delivery
- Props: none
- Takes: 3. Tone matters more here than anywhere else. Not salesy.

**Script — 18 seconds**

| Time | Line |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00 | "'I've got a toddler, I can't go to university.' That's the one I hear most." |
| 0:05 | "There's a Childcare Grant. It covers 85% of your childcare costs, up to a hundred and ninety-nine pounds sixty-two a week for one child." |
| 0:12 | "Three hundred and forty-two twenty-four for two or more. And you don't pay it back." |
| 0:16 | "Most people have never heard of it." |

**On-screen text frames**
1. `0:00` — "I've got a toddler. I can't."
2. `0:05` — Childcare Grant: 85% of costs
3. `0:09` — up to £199.62/wk — 1 child
4. `0:12` — up to £342.24/wk — 2+ children
5. `0:16` — You don't pay it back

**Second script — Parents' Learning Allowance, 12 seconds**

| Time | Line |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00 | "There's a second one nobody mentions." |
| 0:02 | "Parents' Learning Allowance — between fifty pounds and two thousand and twenty-four a year, depending on your household income." |
| 0:09 | "Also not repaid. Also barely advertised." |

**B-roll:** a parent's hands packing a child's bag, or a buggy by a front door.
Keep children's faces out of frame — you do not want a consent problem.

## SB-08 — Psychology and counselling

**Evidence:** Tier 1.5. A competitor launched four separate ads behind this single
vertical between March and May 2026 — that is what finding a winner looks like.
UNIADS can serve it: Arden offers *BA (Hons) Psychology and Counselling with
Foundation Year* and *BA (Hons) Criminology and Psychology with Foundation Year*.

**Feeds:** 3 posts

**Setup**
- Location: desk
- Framing: chest up
- Props: none
- Takes: 2 per variant

**Script — experience as entry evidence, 16 seconds**

| Time | Line |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00 | "If you've spent years looking after people — as a carer, a support worker, a parent — you already have the thing universities are looking for." |
| 0:07 | "Psychology and counselling is one of the routes our partners run with a foundation year attached." |
| 0:12 | "Which means you can start it without A-Levels, and study around a job." |

**Script — career changer, 14 seconds**

| Time | Line |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00 | "The most common thing people want to retrain into? Something that helps people." |
| 0:05 | "Psychology and counselling, criminology and psychology — both available with a foundation year." |
| 0:10 | "Two days a week. You keep working while you do it." |

**On-screen text frames**
- `Years of caring for people = evidence` / `BA (Hons) Psychology & Counselling` / `with Foundation Year` / `No A-Levels needed`

## SB-09 — Meet your counsellor

**Evidence:** Tier 3.4. Competitors' verified Google reviews repeatedly name one
advisor and credit him personally — people buy a human, not an agency. UNIADS has
the stronger version: a named counsellor with a publicly verifiable British
Council training badge. Neither competitor names an accountable person anywhere.

**Feeds:** 3 posts, plus the pinned profile video on TikTok and Instagram

**Setup**
- Location: office, real desk, some depth behind you
- Framing: slightly wider than the rest — this one should feel like meeting someone
- Props: the badge on screen, and the verification URL
- Takes: 4. This is the pinned video; it needs to be the warmest take you have.

**Script — 20 seconds**

| Time | Line |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00 | "If you message UNIADS, you're talking to me. Anthony." |
| 0:03 | "I'm a British Council trained counsellor — trained through their Agent and Counsellor Training Hub." |
| 0:08 | "You don't have to take my word for it. The badge has a verification link. Go and check it." |
| 0:13 | "My job is to tell you whether this works for you, including when the answer is no." |
| 0:18 | "That's it. That's the pitch." |

**On-screen text frames**
1. `0:00` — Anthony
2. `0:03` — UK Knowledge Trained Counsellor
3. `0:05` — British Council Agent & Counsellor Training Hub
4. `0:08` — Verify it yourself → enetbadges.com
5. `0:13` — Including when the answer is no

**B-roll:** slow push on the badge graphic; screen recording of the verification
page loading.

**Compliance note — read this twice.** The claim is *"British Council trained
counsellor"*, describing training held by a named individual. Never "British
Council accredited / approved / partnered / endorsed". CAP 3.52 prohibits claiming
approval or endorsement by a body that has not given it, and the ASA has upheld
exactly that kind of complaint. See guardrails 2.6.

## SB-12 — Student stories (UGC)

**Evidence:** A competitor's 95 Google reviews at 4.9/5 are their single strongest
asset, and UNIADS currently has no published social proof at all. This is the
biggest gap to close, and the one block you cannot self-produce.

**Feeds:** 3+ posts per student

Do not film this alongside the other blocks. It needs real students, real consent
and a different process — see [ugc-brief.md](ugc-brief.md) for the questions, the
consent form and the compliance rules. CAP 3.47 requires documentary evidence that
a testimonial is genuine plus contact details for the person giving it, and CAP
3.50 requires permission.

Until a real student has been captured under that process, **no testimonial
content ships.** No stock footage of graduates presented as UNIADS students, no
invented names. A competitor's placeholder-looking testimonials ("James Anderson,
Student", no course, no photo) are a cautionary example, not a template.
